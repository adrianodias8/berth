DDEV + Drupal CMS on berth (two runs): composer install 9.0 / 9.1 s
(OrbStack 6.6 s), drush site:install 46.4 / 40.0 s (37 s), first page 2.5 s
(2.0 s), warm page 15-17 ms (14 ms).

- **A bug the benchmark hid:** the first run's bind-mount extract (0.32 s)
  was busybox tar failing. It creates files with O_CREAT|O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW,
  and macOS rejects O_NOFOLLOW together with the O_NOFOLLOW_ANY berth adds
  (EINVAL). Fixed; compare.sh now reports a failed step instead of timing it.
- **Idle memory:** Podman, nerdctl and BuildKit, when on, add ~570 MB
  (+1936 MB). With them off, +1366 MB against +1022 MB the day before; the
  system-wide figure is noisy (other apps), so only the engines' share is
  clear.
- **Idle CPU:** ~0.8 points above the baseline. The guest agent's loops
  forked `systemctl` every 5 s (Podman's and k3s's state); now they check
  files. The listener poll for host networking costs ~0.3 % of one vCPU.
- **Throughput** is up from the baseline: the tunnel device for
  host.docker.internal and wider vsock buffers.
